## 5. Suggested simulation parameters derived from the above

- One single-line block section, Tadali ↔ Ghugus, length 14.11 km.
- Terminal at Ghugus: two sidings (GSG colliery siding, 4 km; CESG ACC cement siding, 2 km). Model each as a loading berth with its own placement/release times.
- Terminal at Tadali: junction with the double (becoming triple) Wardha–Balharshah main line; empties arrive from and loads depart to the main line.
- Loop counts at both ends are unknown — treat as a sensitivity parameter (1–3 loops at Ghugus, 2–4 at Tadali).
- Running time per block occupation: 14.11 km at 25–40 km/h ≈ 21–34 min, plus block clearance/signalling overhead.
