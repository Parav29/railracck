## 3. Implications for the simulation

- Baseline scenario: one single-line block Tadali–Ghugus. Doubling scenario: two independent block lines (one per direction), same terminals.
- The officially expected gain is +4.5 MTPA ≈ +3 loaded rakes/day; the model's capacity-gain estimate can be benchmarked against this.
- No official current throughput figure for the spur exists in public sources; WCL siding figures (see wcl_ghugus_loading.md) are the best proxy.
