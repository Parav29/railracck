# Indian Railways norms: running-staff (loco pilot / guard) duty hours, rest, crew change

Purpose: parameters for the crew-availability constraint in the coal rake-cycle DES:
maximum continuous duty, extension rule, rest at HQ/outstation, sign-on lead time and
pre-departure detention.

Legal frame: Railway Servants (Hours of Work and Period of Rest) Rules, 2005 ("HOER"),
under the Railways Act 1989; running staff are classified "Continuous" (54 h/week
statutory, 104 h/fortnight rostered, 108 h statutory).
* IRIMEE HOER note: https://rskr.irimee.in/sites/default/files/HOER.pdf
* Ministry of Labour brief on HOER 2005: https://labour.gov.in/node/88748
* IREM Chapter 15 (Rule 1511, Breach of Rest Allowance):
  https://indianrailways.gov.in/railwayboard/uploads/directorate/establishment/estbl-chap15-p01-p16.htm

## 1. Duty hours at a stretch

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Running duty at a stretch "should not ordinarily exceed" | **9 h** | RBE 143/2016, Board letter E(LL)/2016/HPC/1 dated 28.11.2016 (HPC decisions) https://www.airfindia.org/2016/12/04/recommendations-of-the-high-power-committeehpc-to-review-the-duty-hours-of-running-staff-decisions-thereof-rbe-1432016/ |
| Extension beyond 9 h | permitted only if crew given **at least 2 hours' notice** before the 9 h expire | RBE 143/2016 (same) |
| Total duty sign-on to sign-off | **should not exceed 11 h** | RBE 143/2016 (same) |
| Absolute cap "subject to severe operational exigencies" | **12 h** sign-on to sign-off | Railway Board consolidated guidelines Oct 2023 (Tribune 14.10.2023) https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/drivers-shouldnt-work-beyond-12-hours-railyway-board-553087 |
| Older zonal norm (NR Operating Manual App. B, pre-2016) | 10 h running duty; 8% of 23,835 crews exceeded 10 h (Dec 2014-Feb 2015) | CAG Report 47 of 2015 para 2.4.6 https://cag.gov.in/webroot/uploads/download_audit_report/2015/Union_Performance_Railways_Crew_mgmt_Report_47_2015.pdf |
| Fortnightly ceiling | **125 duty hours per fortnight** | RBE 120/2016, E(LL)/2016/HPC/7 dated 13.10.2016 https://www.gconnect.in/orders-in-brief/railways-orders-in-brief/decision-high-power-committee-duty-hours-running-staff.html |
| Continuous night duties | max **4 nights**, 4th night towards HQ | RBE 120/2016 (same) |
| Away from HQ | max **72 h**; >36 h monitored at division | RBE 120/2016 (same) |
| Union position (AILRSA, Aug 2025) | reiterates 9 h stretch / 11 h total; alleges CMS "fake breaks" | https://www.deccanherald.com/india/loco-pilots-union-alleges-manipulation-of-working-hours-to-hide-overutilisation-3662354 |

## 2. Rest rules

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Rest at headquarters after duty >= 8 h | **16 h** | IREM Rule 1511 https://indianrailways.gov.in/railwayboard/uploads/directorate/establishment/estbl-chap15-p01-p16.htm |
| Rest at headquarters after duty < 8 h | **12 h** | IREM Rule 1511 (same) |
| Outstation rest after 8 h duty | **8 h** | Railway Board circular to CRIS dated 03.06.2025 (standardising to three types) https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Frailway-ministry-changes-outstation-rest-rules-for-train-crew-union-calls-it-unlawful-order-3576029 |
| Outstation rest after 5-8 h duty | **6 h** | same |
| Outstation rest after <= 5 h duty | duty + 1 h | same |
| Periodic rest | 4 x 30 consecutive h or 5 x 22 consecutive h per month, incl. a full night (22:00-06:00) | HOER note https://rskr.irimee.in/sites/default/files/HOER.pdf |
| HPC 2016 view | outstation rest below 8 h "highly inadequate" | Deccan Herald article above |

## 3. Crew change / relief when duty expires en route

* RBE 143/2016 clause 3 covers the case where the train does not reach destination or
  the normal crew-changing / reliever point within the limit: the administration must
  arrange relief (the 2 h notice rule applies to extending the current crew). Text:
  https://www.airfindia.org/2016/12/04/recommendations-of-the-high-power-committeehpc-to-review-the-duty-hours-of-running-staff-decisions-thereof-rbe-1432016/
* Practice reported by CAG 47/2015 (crew management): crew changing points are
  nominated per division; relief crews are "called" from the running room / lobby via
  CMS. No Board-wide time standard for relief-crew arrival; model it as a wait on the
  crew lobby queue plus travel (road van) to the stabled train.
  https://cag.gov.in/webroot/uploads/download_audit_report/2015/Union_Performance_Railways_Crew_mgmt_Report_47_2015.pdf
* Freight trains whose crew expire en route are stabled at the nearest station until a
  relief crew arrives ("crew-expired detention"); this is reported in Hitavada (Dec
  2024) in the context of Railway Board scrutiny of continuous night duties:
  https://www.thehitavada.com/Encyc/2024/12/29/Railway-Board-raises-eyebrows-over-staff-s-continuous-night-shifts.html

## 4. Sign-on lead time, calling, pre-departure detention (CAG Report 47 of 2015, CMS data)

Source: https://cag.gov.in/webroot/uploads/download_audit_report/2015/Union_Performance_Railways_Crew_mgmt_Report_47_2015.pdf

* Para 2.1.12: crew must sign on **10 to 45 minutes before scheduled departure**
  (train-ordering time). In 2,771,169 transactions 26% signed on at/after ordering
  time and 12% signed on < 10 min before it.
* Para 2.1.10: calling time -- 21.52% of calls made > 165 min before ordering time;
  6.66% after it.
* Para 2.1.11: call acknowledgement -- 54.21% within 30 min; 16.07% >= 165 min; CMS
  manual standard 10 min for SMS calls.
* Para 2.1.13: sign-on approval -- 68% approved >= 30 min after sign-on.
* Para 2.1.14: sign-off approval -- 37% approved > 1 h after sign-off.
* CMS defines **Pre-Departure Detention (PDD)** = later of (sign-on, booked departure)
  -> actual departure. https://cms.indianrail.gov.in/CMSHelp/2.Freight/PDD.html
* CAG Report 31 of 2014 para 3.4: of 224,599 late-started goods trains at 119
  points (2010-13), ~63% were late for want of locos and 18% for want of crew and
  path.
  https://cag.gov.in/uploads/download_audit_report/2014/Union_Performance_Railways_Management_Goods_trains_31_2014_chapter_7.pdf

## 5. Suggested parameters for the model

| Quantity | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Max running duty before relief needed | 9 h (planning); hard cap 11 h; 12 h exigency | RBE 143/2016; RB 2023 |
| Extension allowed if notice given >= 2 h before 9 h | up to 11 h total | RBE 143/2016 |
| Sign-on to departure (crew booking + loco/BPC check) | tri(0.5, 0.75, 2.0) h | CAG: 10-45 min sign-on lead + 68% approvals >30 min + PDD |
| Sign-off | tri(0.25, 0.5, 1.0) h | CAG 37% approvals > 1 h |
| Outstation rest before next duty | 6 h (duty 5-8 h) / 8 h (duty >= 8 h) | RB 03.06.2025 |
| HQ rest before next duty | 12 h (<8 h duty) / 16 h (>= 8 h duty) | IREM 1511 |
| Relief-crew arrival when crew expires en route | tri(1, 2.5, 6) h | no published norm; CAG late-start and detention data; treat as calibratable |
| Night duty cap | 4 consecutive nights | RBE 120/2016 |
